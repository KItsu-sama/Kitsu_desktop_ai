# Legacy Kitsu Reference Summary

Legacy Kitsu represents earlier architectural attempts and design explorations for the Kitsu AI companion system.

- **Component Reference**: Detailed technical specifications for subsystems
- **Implementation Patterns**: Proven solutions for common problems
- **Performance Insights**: Optimization strategies and benchmarks
- **Feature Specifications**: Detailed requirements for quiz system, emotion engine, etc.
